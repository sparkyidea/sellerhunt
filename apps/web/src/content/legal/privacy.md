## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account information**: name, email address, password (stored hashed), and any optional profile details such as a display name, username, or avatar.
- **Authentication credentials**: passkeys (public key only) and session tokens used to keep you signed in.
- **Billing information**: if you subscribe to a paid plan, billing details are processed by our payment processor; we store only the metadata needed to manage your subscription.
- **Support communications**: messages you send us and any attachments.

### 2.2 Information from connected marketplaces
When you connect an eBay or Shopify account, we receive and store:
- **OAuth tokens** (access and refresh tokens) issued by the marketplace, used to call the marketplace's API on your behalf. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
- **Catalog data**: products, variants, SKUs, prices, inventory levels, images, categories.
- **Order data**: order line items, customer ship-to addresses, fulfillment status, shipping labels, returns, and related metadata.
- **Shop and account metadata**: shop name, shop ID, marketplace user ID, currency, and similar settings.

We only request the OAuth scopes needed to provide the Service. You can disconnect a marketplace account at any time from your Dashseller settings.

### 2.3 Information collected automatically
- **Device and log data**: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and timestamps.
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies) for details.

### 2.4 Information we do not collect
- We do not collect information from children under 13.
- We do not knowingly process special categories of personal data (such as biometric, health, or precise geolocation data) and ask that you not submit such information through the Service.

- Respond to support requests;
- Monitor usage, debug issues, and improve the Service;
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service;
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 8).

When you disconnect a marketplace, we delete the OAuth tokens for that connection. Catalog and order data synced before disconnection may be retained in your account so historical records remain available, until you delete the account.

## 7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for sensitive fields such as marketplace OAuth tokens, hashed passwords, scoped access controls, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Correction**: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion**: ask us to delete your information.

- **Opt-out**: opt out of certain processing (see Section 9 for California specifics).
- **Withdraw consent**: where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@dashseller.com from the address on your account. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30–45 days).

## 9. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA"), gives you rights to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of your personal information.

- **Categories of personal information collected**: identifiers (name, email, IP address), commercial information (subscription and order data synced from your marketplaces), internet or network activity, and inferences drawn from the foregoing.
- **Sale or sharing**: we do **not** sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- **Sensitive personal information**: we use sensitive personal information (such as account credentials) only for the purposes permitted by Section 7027(m) of the CCPA regulations.
- **Right to non-discrimination**: we will not deny you service or charge you a different price for exercising your rights.

To exercise California rights, email privacy@dashseller.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request." We may need to verify your identity before responding.
