**Last updated: May 3, 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how Sparky Idea Inc. ("Dashseller," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on the Dashseller website and dashboard (the "Service"). It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

By continuing to use the Service, you consent to the use of cookies as described below, except where consent is required by law and you have not given it.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to store information. We also use related technologies such as `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, and pixel tags. We refer to all of these as "cookies" in this Policy for simplicity.

Cookies can be **first-party** (set by Dashseller) or **third-party** (set by services we use). They can be **session** cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or **persistent** cookies (kept until they expire or you delete them).

## 2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes.

### 2.1 Strictly necessary
These cookies are required to operate the Service and cannot be turned off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as signing in, setting privacy preferences, or filling in forms.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication session | Keeps you signed in to your Dashseller account | Session / up to 30 days |
| CSRF token | Prevents cross-site request forgery | Session |
| Auth state | Tracks sign-in flow and account selection | Session |

### 2.2 Preferences
These cookies remember choices you make so the Service behaves the way you expect.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `theme` | Remembers your light/dark theme preference | 1 year |
| Locale / timezone | Remembers your display preferences | 1 year |

### 2.3 Analytics (where enabled)
If we enable analytics, these cookies help us understand how the Service is used so we can improve it. We configure analytics to minimize personal data and do not use them for advertising. If we add analytics, we will update this Policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

### 2.4 What we do not use
We do **not** use cookies for cross-site advertising, behavioral profiling, or selling your information to third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

The Service may load resources from trusted third parties (for example, our authentication, error monitoring, or content delivery providers) that set their own cookies. We list our subprocessors in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

If you connect a marketplace (eBay or Shopify), the OAuth flow may briefly involve cookies set by that marketplace's domain. Those cookies are governed by the marketplace's own privacy and cookie policies.

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

- **Browser settings.** Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies. See your browser's help center for instructions (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may break sign-in and other core features.
- **Device settings.** Mobile devices have similar controls; see your device's privacy settings.
- **Opt-out signals.** We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where required by law. You can enable GPC in supported browsers.

## 5. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no industry standard for how to interpret these signals, we do not respond to them, but we honor GPC as described above.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. If changes are material, we will notify you through the Service.
